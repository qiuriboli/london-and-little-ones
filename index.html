Bank Holiday Edition

A long weekend of unicorns, cardboard Macbeths, and chickens to chat to.

The first bank holiday of the season has landed, and London has rolled out a programme so chaotic and joyful that picking is genuinely hard. We've sifted through this week's three best newsletters — Loopla, Dads in London and The London Scoop — plus a sweep of the wider web, and pulled out everything worth your Saturday.

Sunshine forecast. Marathon Sunday is behind us. Three full days ahead.

No. 01

Editors' picks for the bank holiday

Headline events

Six events we'd actually move plans for. Each pulled from our trusted sources and verified across the web. Tap to read more on the venue's site.

Bank Holiday Outdoor All ages

Go Green Fest at Fulham Palace

Reimagined from the old Green Meet into a hands-on day for kids: woodworking, eco crafts, "Guardians of the Rainforest" sessions, bee walks, chicken chats (yes, really), seed planting, bottle upcycling, and the first face-painting of the season. 29 activities, all but one suitable for children.

📅
Sunday 3 May, 11:00–16:00
📍
Fulham Palace, Bishop's Avenue, SW6 6EA
🎟
Adults £5, children £3
Featured in Dads in London + The London Scoop
Bank Holiday Theatre Ages 7–11

MakeBeth — Macbeth in cardboard

Shakespeare's darkest tragedy reimagined as a "cardBARD" make-along. Audiences help build the witches' cauldron ingredients, masks for Banquo's ghost, and Birnam Wood itself. Three performers act, play music live, and keep things just silly enough to dodge nightmare territory.

📅
Sunday 3 May, 11:00 & 14:00
📍
artsdepot, Tally Ho Corner, N12 0GA
🎟
£13.75 per person
Featured in Dads in London + The London Scoop
FREE Bank Holiday All ages

Canalway Cavalcade at Little Venice

Dozens of canal boats moor up in Little Venice for three days of live music, children's activities, and an illuminated boat parade in the evening. One of the loveliest, most chilled-out free outings of the bank holiday — a real picnic-by-the-water kind of day.

📅
Saturday 2 – Monday 4 May
📍
Little Venice, W2
🎟
Free
Sourced from Londonist + Loopla
Bank Holiday Animals All ages

London Zoo Big Birthday Bash

Part of ZSL's 200th anniversary, a family street party across the zoo split into four zones: Party Animals (live music), the Winning Zone (fairground games), Creation Station (crafts), and the Birthday Banquet (food stalls). Included with zoo entry. Zoo lodges available too if you fancy sleeping with the lions.

📅
Saturday 2 – Monday 4 May
📍
London Zoo, Regent's Park, NW1
🎟
Included with zoo entry
Featured in Loopla + Londonist
Cinema Ages 5+

Family Film Club: Finding Dory

Pixar's amnesiac fish on the big screen, ten years on. The Barbican's Family Film Club gives the kids cinema seats, snacks, and a properly turned-down volume. £2.50 for under-18s makes this the cheapest morning out of the weekend by some margin.

📅
Saturday 2 May, 11:00
📍
Barbican Centre, Silk Street, EC2Y 8DS
🎟
Adults £5, under-18s £2.50
Featured in Dads in London
Science Ages 6+

Mathmagics! at the Royal Institution

David Hall's interactive maths show in the same Mayfair lecture theatre where Faraday demonstrated electromagnetic induction. Tricks, stories and facts spanning centuries — pitched to delight maths-lovers and convert maths-avoiders. Selling out fast; new tickets being released.

📅
Saturday 2 May
📍
Royal Institution, Mayfair, W1S
🎟
From £10
Featured in The London Scoop
